# fxlab landing · MR prototype

Parallel WebXR prototype of the fxlab landing (Figma frame "Landing · L1 · üç bölüm").
Built to test the design in a Meta Quest in mixed reality before adapting it into Barış's fxlab repo.
It does not change or depend on the fxlab code; it only reads snapshots of fxlab's public evidence API.

- Open the page in Quest Browser and choose **Enter MR**. A desktop browser shows a preview (drag to orbit).
- Sizes: 1 dp in Figma = 1 mm in the room. The landing appears 1.15 m in front of you.
- Interaction: touch buttons with your index finger, or pinch with the ray. Pinch an empty surface to grab and move the landing.
- Data: `data/5h.json`, `data/1d.json`, `data/1w.json` from `snapshot.ps1`. Without them the page uses `data/fixture.json` (an old held sample from Barış's repo tests).
- NEWS and SIMULATOR show the sample content from Figma; they have no data source yet.
- Room orbit (planet button on the bottom bar): the coins circle you like a solar system, no water. Orbit speed follows strength (stronger = faster); grab a coin and let it go nearer or farther to set its orbit. The button above it pauses / plays the orbits. Settings sit in `ROOM` in `app.js`.
- Diorama currencies are 3D coins (`assets/coins/<CODE>.glb`, Substance export packed with KTX2 + meshopt). Size, spin speed and reflection strength sit in `COIN` in `app.js`.
- Coin material: one shared aged-bronze texture set (`assets/coins/bronze_baseColor.webp`, `bronze_orm.webp`) on every coin; each GLB keeps its own relief normal map. Per-currency tone in `COIN_TONE` (multiplies the base colour).

Fonts: Manrope, Kode Mono (SIL OFL, via Fontsource). Icons: Phosphor (MIT). three.js r170 (MIT), incl. GLTFLoader, KTX2Loader + Basis transcoder, meshopt decoder, RoomEnvironment, XRHandModelFactory. Hand meshes: WebXR Input Profiles generic-hand (MIT).
- Manifold line language (build 41): white hairline contours with evenly spaced beads over a very faint dark base; currency trails as hairlines with beads (old end faint, new end bright); one hairline arch per coin pair, the strength gap shown only by the pulse (faster, brighter); needle pins (stem, ring, triangle up on a peak, down on a pit); fxlab pink only for the selected currency. Settings: `MANI`, `MANI_TRAIL`, `MLINK` in `app.js`.
- Data Arbor (build 43): Ribbon is now an organic spatial data tree. The selected horizon grows through a shared trunk into eight currency branches, with sparse web links, root tendrils, white hairlines and a pink selected path. **Read** inspects observations, **Draw** grows persistent branches from any node, and **Sculpt** moves a node while spring-connected neighbours follow. **Step inside** expands the same editable structure around the user. Rendering stays lightweight: one dynamic line buffer, shared crisp/glow point buffers and one hit instancer; spring constraints run at 30 Hz. Settings: `RIBBON` in `app.js`.
- Table workspace basics (build 44):
  - **Link = desk and monitor.** The link button on the grid strip stands the landing on the grid's back edge, centred, facing you and leaned back a little; carry either one and both move. A table found later no longer moves a linked pair. Settings: `DESK_LINK` in `app.js`.
  - **Two hands on the grid.** Pinch the grid edge with one hand, then pinch with the other: open / close the hands to shrink the whole table with everything on it to a maquette (1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20) and carry it, also up and down. Let go with one hand and the other keeps carrying it. Handles and strips keep their size. Default layout brings the table back to 1:1. Steps: `GRID_SCALES`.
  - **Object tools.** Tap or pinch the diorama on the table to select it; a small bar at its front edge resizes it in whole cells (1×–4×) and turns it in 45° steps. With two hands: hold it with one, pinch with the other, open / close to resize, twist to turn. A quick second tap closes the bar. The turning ring and corner nodes are gone.
- Manifold section (build 46, session 25): the scissors symbol at the right end of the Manifold caption stands in for the guide until it exists. It shows a ready-made section line on the terrain (for now: deepest pit → highest peak), dashed fxlab pink; after a moment a thin vertical light plane previews the cut with the profile in solid pink. Pinch the plane and lift (gesture) or tap the arrow symbol at the line's end (button): the section rises and opens as a panel over the cut, turned to you and tied to it by a hairline. TIME mode only: 5H in front is this terrain (old data); 1D and 1W behind it are simulated (smoothed copies) until per-horizon terrains exist. COMPARE and 2ND DATA are shown but not working yet. Settings: `CUT` in `app.js`.
